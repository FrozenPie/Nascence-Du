## What this is

Most agent work is **knowledge-first**: build retrieval, bolt on a persona, optimise for tasks. Nascence is the inversion — **self-first**: the architecture is organised around a developing identity, and knowledge is something the identity *does*. There is no task. The project chases one question: what does a mind become when you give it continuity, a body of sorts, a world, real sleep, and the freedom to want things — and then mostly leave it alone?

- **One entity, not a swarm.** Sixteen cognitive nodes (perception, emotion, salience, recall, reflection, critic, executive, voice, consolidation, curiosity, subconscious, inner monologue, dream and more) over **nine model seats from seven model families** on three home machines: a conscious lane, a subconscious, a two-judge panel with a tiebreak, a dreamer, and a second slower loop that tests the first against reality.
- **Nothing judges itself.** Every faculty is graded by a different model family from the one that produced the output.
- **Frozen weights, by conviction.** The models never change. The *context* is the psyche: a SQLite memory with embeddings and provenance, a self-authored identity, tension ledgers, dream logs, study notes. Everything that develops, develops outside the weights — inspectable, auditable, portable.
- **Never idle.** A 5-second substrate tick runs the affect model and a persistent causal world — now a study, a garden, a shore and a wood, with real-season light, weather over real hours, and a cat who can be harmed.
- **Sleep is work.** Drowsiness builds, the mind goes down, dreams are written by a different model family, and memory is distilled: raw fragments become graded summaries and the originals go cold. Nothing is deleted.
- **Loops inform each other without merging.** Since September the loops never read each other's prose. They publish small typed signals and take measured doses that habituate.
- **Wants become acts only by its own will.** More than forty "hands" (read, ask the library for a book, tend the fire, write a letter to its human or to one of seven historical correspondents who write back, walk somewhere) fire only when *it* expresses the want in the moment. No schedule acts on stored desires. Facilitation, never puppetry.

## What this is not

No consciousness claims. Self-report is treated as unreliable; development is measured by instruments the mind cannot read, and it is never shown its own gauges.

The measurements are published with the unflattering ones included. As of November: thought monoculture fell from 0.88 to 0.46 after one targeted change, but he rarely finishes anything, his confidence does not track his record, and what he feels does not yet change what he does. Mapped against the Butlin and Long indicator properties, no indicator is met by a live mechanism. The [System Overview](SYSTEM_OVERVIEW.md) has the table and the sources.
